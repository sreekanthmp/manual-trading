How to use this page before a manual trade: read top to bottom, it takes a few seconds
1. Is the data safe? The header must say MARKET OPEN, FEED CONNECTED and DATA TRUSTED. A red or grey chip (UNKNOWN means the page lost its link), or a red alert line under the tabs (click it for every alert), means do not trade on this screen.
2. What is the decision? Only BUY CE or BUY PE means a setup passed the rules. WAIT and NO TRADE mean stay out; the line beside it says why.
3. Can I enter now? The Entry line must say the setup is eligible right now. "Not eligible" means the setup is confirmed but price is not in the entry area, or something blocks it. If the page says STALE, it has lost its link to Prism: nothing on it is current, whatever it shows.
4. Do the timeframes agree? 1H and 15M must point the same way as the side. 5M and 1M are information only.
5. Where is the idea wrong? Current setup shows the trigger, the entry zone and the invalidation level. If price closes beyond the invalidation, the setup is over. You choose your own stop and size.
6. Which option? Only for a BUY: open the Options tab. The contract must be FRESH with a tight spread and no blocking findings. "No suitable contract" means no option trade.
Alerts. The 🔔 button in the header chooses what tells you when a setup becomes ready, stops being ready, or the data stops being trustworthy: sound + popup, sound or off. A sound needs one click on this page first (a browser rule); a popup needs your browser's permission. The log under the tabs keeps the last few.
Your own trades. The Trades tab is a notebook: log what you bought and sold (Prism places nothing and reads no account) and it keeps what Prism said at that moment, shows the day's realised result against a loss limit you set (RISK_MAX_DAILY_LOSS), and sizes a trade to your stop.
7. Then you decide. Prism describes the NIFTY index and places no orders. Enter, size and exit yourself in Angel One. Use Journal afterwards to review what Prism said and why. Analysis holds the detail behind the decision.
